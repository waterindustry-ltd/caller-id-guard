1. Data processed on your device (not sent to our servers)

Caller ID Guard performs its core caller-ID, spam-call-blocking, and dialer functions directly on your device. The following data is read and processed on-device only and is not uploaded to or stored on any server operated by us:

  • Call log (READ_CALL_LOG) — to show your call history (All / Missed / Incoming / Outgoing) and to power caller-ID lookups for incoming/outgoing numbers.
  • Phone state and call control (READ_PHONE_STATE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS, CALL_PHONE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS) — to let the App act as your dialer: place calls, answer or decline calls, and show the in-call screen.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS) — to display caller names from your address book and to let you view, add, or edit contacts from within the App.
  • Notification content (Notification Access) — used by the "Unseen" feature to read incoming message/notification previews without marking them as seen. This data is read and displayed locally and is not transmitted to us.

Because this data never leaves your device to reach servers we operate, we do not collect it, and it is not included in Google Play's Data Safety "collected" declarations for this app. It stays on your phone and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its app data.

Special device roles the App requests

  • Default phone app / in-call service — required so the App can show the in-call screen and act as your dialer.
  • Call-screening service — required so the App can automatically block or reject incoming calls from numbers you add to your block list.
  • Notification listener — required for the "Unseen" message-preview feature described above.
  • Full-screen incoming-call intent — required to show the full-screen incoming-call UI when your device is locked.

You can revoke the default-dialer role, notification access, or any runtime permission at any time from your device Settings; doing so may disable the related feature.
