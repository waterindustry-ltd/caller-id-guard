FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in anywhere in Caller ID Guard. Install it and start.

Is my call log or contacts uploaded anywhere?

No. Your call log, contacts and message previews are processed on your device to power caller ID, blocking and Unseen. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Is anything locked behind a payment?

No. Every feature — caller ID, spam blocking, Number Tracker and Unseen — is free and unlocked. There is no premium tier and no in-app purchases.

Why does it ask to be the default phone app?

Call screening can only automatically reject numbers on your block list when Caller ID Guard is your default phone app. It also enables the full-screen in-call experience.

What is Unseen?

Unseen lets you read incoming message previews on your device without marking them as seen, so you can check a message without sending a read receipt. It uses notification access you grant yourself.

Does the app show ads?

No. This version does not display ads.
